Everything that quietly eats your disk

Six focused tools. Each one scans, groups what it finds by size, and waits for you to decide.

Smart Scan

One click checks system and developer junk together and shows how much you can reclaim.

System Junk

App caches, logs, crash reports, stale temporary files, Mail attachments and the Trash.

Developer Junk

Xcode DerivedData, Homebrew, npm, pnpm, Yarn, pip, Gradle and Cargo caches, plus node_modules in projects you haven't touched in a month.

Large & Old Files

Videos, disk images, archives and installers above a size you pick, filtered by when you last opened them.

Duplicates

Byte-for-byte identical copies, confirmed with SHA-256. The oldest copy is kept, and you can't select every copy by accident.

Space Lens

A clickable map of any folder, sized by what each item actually occupies on disk. Drill in, reveal in Finder or move to Trash.

Built to be careful

A cleaner should never surprise you. DeepCleaner is designed around that.

  • Nothing is removed without review. Every scan ends in a list you check and confirm.
  • Your files go to the Trash. Only caches that apps rebuild on their own can be deleted outright, and you can turn that off.
  • Protected locations stay protected. System folders, your top-level home folders, Keychains, Mail, iCloud and symlink tricks are refused at the last step.
  • Sizes match Finder. Hard links are counted once, and the summary shows how much free space actually changed.
  • Open source and offline. No account, no telemetry, MIT licensed.

Install

Takes about a minute.

  1. Download the latest DMG, open it, and drag DeepCleaner into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click DeepCleaner and choose Open. Builds aren't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once.
  3. Grant Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security so DeepCleaner can see caches, logs and Mail data.

After an update, re-grant Full Disk Access: each build currently carries a new signature. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DeepCleaner.app and open it again.
